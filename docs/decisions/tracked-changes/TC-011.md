## Round 2 (decided by Mats, 11 October): the night rule, the smaller version

**The decision (final form, 22:30).** No "When?" field. A family search when it is dark at
the searched place (after sunset until 00:00, or from 04:30 until sunrise) is planned
automatically for the next morning, 09:00 local there, with the same duration. Between
00:00 and 04:30 local the night page ("The adventures can wait till morning") stays, with
no new action. Kids are still never sent out after dark: the daylight rule is unchanged
and is applied to the morning window against that date's own sun times. The results
page says "It's dark now, so here are ideas for tomorrow morning. Tomorrow, 09:00–10:00."
(or "this morning" between 04:30 and sunrise). A morning result is valid no later than
the start of its window. Solo searches are unchanged.

**Built (green at 22:37; ruff, mypy, import-linter 6 kept, 1602 tests):**

- `core/night.py`: `is_locked`, `next_morning`, `tomorrow_morning`, `night_outcome`
  (pure; tests at 21:00, 23:59, 00:01, 04:29, 04:31 and before sunrise in four zones).
- `planner/plan_outing.py`: `_family_verdict` replaces the direct daylight call on the
  family path: too short → DAYLIGHT_TOO_SHORT; the lock → DAYLIGHT_NIGHT; dark but not
  locked → `_plan_morning`: the run's `planned_start` becomes the next 09:00 local, one
  more forecast is fetched for that window (`_fetch_weather`, counter stage
  `weather_morning`), and the rules run again on it with `ahead=True`, where a still-dark
  morning is DAYLIGHT_NIGHT. The result carries `NoticeId.PLANNED_FOR_MORNING` first and
  `valid_until ≤ planned_start`.
- Daylight for the right day without a contract change: the adapter already takes the
  sunrise and sunset of the local date of the `start` it is asked for. The weather cache
  key is unchanged; `storage/providers.weather_codec` now also requires the cached
  entry's sunrise to fall on the window's local date, in the entry's own timezone, so
  today's entry is never reused for tomorrow's window, including in the Pacific where
  21:00 and the next 09:00 share one UTC date. Cost: at night a family search at a place
  makes two forecast calls, and the two entries alternate under one key.
- `core/errors.NoticeId.PLANNED_FOR_MORNING` (CONTRACTS §2 list updated), `web/copy.py`
  (`NOTICE_MESSAGES[PLANNED_FOR_MORNING]`, `PLANNED_FOR_THIS_MORNING`,
  `MORNING_WINDOW_TOMORROW`, `MORNING_WINDOW_TODAY`), `web/presenters._morning_body`
  (dated in the place's timezone from the result's own window).
- Copy: "near your location" lower case mid-sentence (`ANSWERS_NEAR_YOUR_LOCATION`).

**Tests:** `tests/planner/test_plan_ahead.py` (Europe/Stockholm and Pacific/Auckland:
