## Round 2 (decided by Mats, 11 October): the night rule and "Plan for tomorrow"

**The decision.** The night lock ("The adventures can wait till morning") applies only
between 00:00 and 04:30 local time at the searched place. A family search after sunset
and before 00:00, or between 04:30 and sunrise, plans for the next morning instead
(09:00 local at the place, the same window logic as the explicit "Tomorrow morning"
choice) with the label "It's dark now, so here are ideas for tomorrow morning,
09:00–10:00." Kids are still never sent out after dark: the daylight rule stays; it plans
ahead instead of saying no. The form gains "When? Now / Tomorrow morning" (default Now).
A result for tomorrow is valid until the window starts or the forecast is too old.

**Landed in the 30-minute box (green, 1580 tests):**

- `core/night.py` (new, pure, not yet called by the workflow): `is_locked` (00:00 ≤ local
  < 04:30 at the place), `next_morning` (the next 09:00 local: the coming morning before
  09:00, otherwise the next local day), `tomorrow_morning` (always the next local day, for
  the explicit choice), `night_outcome` (daylight / too_short / lock / plan_ahead).
  `tests/core/test_night.py`: 21:00, 23:59, 00:01, 04:29, 04:31 and before sunrise in
  Europe/Stockholm, America/New_York, Pacific/Auckland and Asia/Kolkata; the lock follows
  the place, not the server; the morning window across midnight and across the 25 October
  2026 clock change; an unknown zone falls back to UTC.
- Copy: "near your location" (lower case mid-sentence) in the answers line and the print
  context; `copy.ANSWERS_NEAR_YOUR_LOCATION`, `presenters._near`. Test in
  `tests/templates/test_tc011.py`.
- Back navigation: `tests/web/test_browser_location.py` submits a search, goes back and
  asserts the busy note is hidden and the submit button is neither busy nor disabled.

**Not built (reported, not half-wired):**

1. The `when` form field (`now` / `tomorrow_morning`): CONTRACTS §7 field table, `forms.py`
   (`PLAN_FIELDS`, `ALLOWED_VALUES`, the fingerprint), `parsing.py`, `Answers`, the tile
   group, `fields_from_answers`, the answers summary.
2. The workflow: `Run.planned_start` for a morning window; the automatic plan-ahead in
   `plan_outing._evaluate` when `night_outcome` says `plan_ahead`; `valid_until` =
   min(window start, forecast fetched + 30 min); the result label "Tomorrow, 09:00–10:00"
   and "It's dark now, …"; the night page's "Plan for tomorrow morning instead" action
   carrying the answers.
3. The one design point that blocked 2 inside the box: `WeatherWindow` carries the
   sunrise and sunset of the local date of the *requested* start only, and the cached
   weather entry is reused for a window it covers, so a morning window would be judged
   against today's sun times. The adapter already fetches two forecast days; the clean
   fix is to carry per-day sunrise/sunset in `WeatherWindow` (adapter, cache codec,
   `core.weather`), then pick tomorrow's for the morning window. That is a contract
   change (CONTRACTS §2) and needs its own round.
