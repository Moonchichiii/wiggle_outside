## Part A: the cards and the notice box

- Under the hook: at most two of the pick's reviewed reasons (`IdeaView.reasons[:2]`) and,
  on a match, "Matches: birds" from the reviewed tag labels (`IdeaView.match_labels`,
  `copy.CARD_MATCH`); the detail page keeps the full sentence. Same data as before.
- One box above the cards (`<section class="wo-notices">`, labelled by a hidden heading):
  the most important line first, the rest as short secondary lines; the box takes the
  lead notice's kind, so a warning that leads still reads as one. A morning plan leads
  (`presenters._lead_first`). "We found N matching ideas" is gone; the count is visible.
- The forecast line always names the day in the place's time: "Forecast for today, …",
  "tomorrow, …", or the date; the checked-at time stays (`copy.WEATHER_WINDOW*`).

## Part C: the Overpass alternative instance

- Live evidence before: the recent `fly logs` window held one search with
  `places stage_timeout=1` at 21:45 UTC: the one attempt used the whole 9 s stage, so the
  permitted retry never ran. Earlier that evening (docs/evidence/live-journey.md) the
  public instance failed after 6.5 s and kumi answered in 0.2 s.
- Now: `WO_OVERPASS_FALLBACK_URL` (default `https://overpass.kumi.systems/api/interpreter`,
  `settings.overpass_fallback_url`); one attempt uses at most half the stage
  (`overpass.ATTEMPT_BUDGET_S` = 4.5 s), so the retry always has time; a 5xx from Overpass
  is a failed attempt (`TransportFailure`) rather than a cooldown, so the retry can run;
  `storage/providers._CachedPlaces` sends the second attempt to the fallback adapter,
  which is the same adapter class, the same User-Agent and the same query with another
  base URL. No rotation beyond that: one retry, one alternative. After: not measured live
  yet (not deployed).

## Part D: the place on the map

- "View place on OpenStreetMap ↗" on the card and the detail page for an idea with a real
  place, to the place's own OSM page built from the Overpass identity
  (`https://www.openstreetmap.org/node|way|relation/<id>`, `presenters.OSM_IDENTITY`);
  a place without a usable OSM id gets no link. No coordinate travels, no route is
  promised, no map is embedded, no CSP change; new tab, `rel="noopener noreferrer"`,
  the hidden context "(opens in a new tab)". Place-free ideas get no link.
- Place-free ideas read "Start nearby · 30 min activity" on the card and "Start nearby ·
  about 30 min activity (estimate)" on the detail page (`copy.TIMES_ACTIVITY_ONLY`,
  `copy.DETAIL_TIMES_ACTIVITY_ONLY`, `IdeaView.times_detail`).

## Part E: "Before you go"

- `core/packing.py`: the rules, keys only; `web/copy.py` the reviewed wording. Groups in
  order: for the weather, for the little ones (essentials, then the optional extras: the
  toy, the treasure bag), for these ideas (the catalogue's optional items). At most 10
  items, taken in that order. Equivalent items are deduplicated across groups by a key
  (nappies include wipes; extra water and a water bottle each; one hat item; jackets and
  windproof; a catalogue "bag" against the treasure bag).
- Weather: rain ≥ 40 % or rain/snow → waterproof jackets and wellies; the window's lowest
  temperature < 10 °C → a warm layer; < 5 °C → hats and gloves; wind ≥ 25 km/h →
  windproof; ≥ 20 °C and clear → sun hats and cream; ≥ 24 °C → extra water. The summary
  carries no apparent temperature, so the window's lowest temperature stands in for it.
- Snack: cold / mild / warm / rain lines as decided; two hours or more adds a sandwich;
  with a 0–2 selected the only line is "Your usual age-appropriate snacks and drinks."
  and no food item is listed for the youngest. Solo: the weather group, "A charged phone",
  coffee. No line names grapes, nuts or popcorn (tested). Native checkboxes, nothing saved.
- Ends with "A quick reminder, not a complete list."
