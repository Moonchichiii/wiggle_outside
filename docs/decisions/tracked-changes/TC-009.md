# TC-009 — Deadline budget 3 / 9 / 6 / 2, measured, with both Overpass attempts in the places stage

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Change | D32's split inside the 20 s deadline becomes geocode 3 s, places and weather 9 s, model including its queue wait 6 s, 2 s reserved for fallback assembly and rendering. The two Overpass attempts (the one permitted retry, CONTRACTS §4.1) share the 9 s: an attempt may use what is left of the stage, so the retry only happens when the first attempt failed early. The absolute 20 s deadline stays authoritative when earlier stages use up their share: every stage still gets `min(its budget, what is left)`, every call is now cancelled at its stage deadline by the workflow (`plan_outing._bounded`) even if an adapter overran its own bound, and a job is ended as failed at most 1 s past its deadline as a last resort (`jobs.DEADLINE_GRACE_S`). |
| Reason | The 3 / 4 / 11 split was never measured. Measured on 11 October 2026 (docs/evidence/live-journey.md): a Växjö places query took 2.1 to 5.1 s when the public Overpass instance was healthy and 6 s or more under load; a 4 s stage (and a 3 s per-attempt cap, tried first) lost most searches, and the retry never had time to run. The model answered in 1.68 s with no queue (docs/evidence/model-smoke.md), so 6 s including the slot wait leaves ample margin. |
